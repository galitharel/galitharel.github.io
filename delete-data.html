Delete your data:
The I Love Method™

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account and your data from The I Love Method™ app, operated by Faith St. (SASU), 420 chemin des Cambreniers, 06140 Vence, France.

How to delete your account

Write to contact@theilovemethod.com from the email address you use for your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete your account and your data within 30 days and we confirm by email when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account details: email address, name, year of birth, gender, country, language, time zone, and your preferred form of address.
  • Your questionnaire and check-in answers and your profile.
  • Your conversations with the AI Companion.
  • Your personalized scripts and audio.
  • Your voice recordings.
  • Your consent records and your AI usage record (counters only).
  • Your notification token, if you allowed notifications.

What is kept

  • Copies in backups and server logs, for up to seven days. Server logs may contain an account identifier, an IP address and request times.
  • Information that we are legally required or permitted to keep under applicable law.
  • Anonymous, aggregated statistics that cannot identify you.

The AI services we use (Anthropic, OpenAI, ElevenLabs) may keep data for a limited period under their own terms, as explained in our Privacy Policy.

Delete only some of your data

You can delete your voice recordings in the app, on the My Recordings screen. To ask us to delete other data without deleting your account, write to contact@theilovemethod.com.
