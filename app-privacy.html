Privacy Policy:
The I Love Method™

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use The I Love Method™ app.

The I Love Method™ is operated by Faith St., a French simplified joint-stock company with a sole shareholder (SASU), registered with the Trade and Companies Register (RCS) of Grasse under number 978 793 131, with its registered office at 420 chemin des Cambreniers, 06140 Vence, France.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account information: name, email address, password, year of birth (we use it to check that you are 18 or older), gender, country, language, and your time zone (detected by your phone).
  • Questionnaire responses and emotional profile data.
  • Messages you write to the AI Companion and the conversation history with it.
  • Voice recordings that you choose to create in your own voice using the My Voice feature.
  • Your preferred form of address, including feminine or masculine forms where required by the language.
  • Your consent choices: what you agreed to, which version of the text, and when.
  • Communications with our support team, including messages sent through the contact form.
  • Reports you send about an AI reply, using the Report button: the text of the reply (which can contain your first name and details from your answers), the feature it came from, the language, the time, your account identifier, and the note you choose to add. Your own messages and your profile are not sent with a report.

Information collected automatically

  • Your activity in the app, such as the audio you listen to, your check-ins and your progress.
  • Server logs, including IP addresses, timestamps, and error logs.
  • A usage record for the AI services: for each use, which service, which feature, the amount of text processed (measured in tokens or characters), and the date and time. This record never contains the content of your messages, scripts, or recordings.
  • A push-notification token, if you allow notifications, used to send you reminders.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To personalize your experience based on your questionnaire responses and preferences.
  • To generate personalized audio content and provide AI-assisted support, which involves sending some of your information to the AI service providers named in section 5.
  • To adapt content to your preferences and the information you provide, and to track your progress in the app over time.
  • To improve, maintain, and optimize the platform's performance and security.
  • To work out the cost of the AI services and to prevent abuse, using the usage record described above.
  • To review the reports you send about AI replies, which we read within 3 days of receiving them, and to keep the service safe.
  • To send communications related to the operation of the app, your account, and the service. Communications that are not essential to the service will be sent in accordance with your preferences and applicable law.
  • To improve the service using aggregated, de-identified statistics, such as which features are used and how often. We do not read your conversations to improve the service, and we do not use the content of your messages or recordings to build or train AI models.
  • To comply with our legal obligations.

Access to the content of your conversations is limited to what is strictly necessary for security, to comply with a legal obligation, or to answer a support request that you make.

3. Legal Bases for Processing

We process personal information on one or more of the following legal bases, depending on the purpose of the processing and applicable law:

  • Consent: where your consent is required, including when you agree to share your text with AI service providers.
  • Contractual necessity: where processing is necessary to provide the services you have requested.
  • Legitimate interests: where processing is necessary for purposes such as platform security, fraud prevention, cost control, and service improvement, provided your rights and interests are respected.
  • Legal obligation: where we are required to process information to comply with the law.

Where applicable law requires an additional legal basis or a specific condition for processing certain categories of personal information, we will comply with those requirements.

4. Voice Recordings

The recordings covered by this section are recordings that you create in your own voice using the My Voice feature for your personal use within the app.

  • Your recordings are stored on the app's server, separately from AI services.
  • The AI Companion has no access to these recordings.
  • Your recordings are not sent to AI providers and are not used to train AI models.
  • Your recordings are not sold or used for advertising.
  • You can delete your recordings through the My Recordings screen in the app.
  • Access to recording files stored on the server is restricted to authorized Faith St. personnel where access is necessary for app operation, support, or security, and in accordance with applicable access controls.

This section applies only to your personal voice recordings. Conversations with the AI Companion and other data processing within the app are governed by the relevant provisions of this Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing Information

We do not sell your personal information.

AI service providers. To provide the AI Companion and your personalized audio, the app sends some of your information to these providers, which process it on our behalf:

  • Anthropic, PBC (United States): the text of your messages to the AI Companion and the information used to write your personalized scripts (such as your questionnaire and check-in answers, your gender, your preferred form of address and your language). It generates the replies and scripts.
  • OpenAI, L.L.C. (United States): the Hebrew text of replies and scripts, which can contain your first name and details from your answers, to refine the Hebrew wording. Because Hebrew is written in a masculine or feminine form, the form of address used for you can be seen in that text.
  • ElevenLabs (United States): the text of your personalized scripts, to turn them into audio.

We send only what is needed for each feature. We do not send your voice recordings from the My Voice feature to any of them. You agree to this sharing on the consent screen before the AI features are used, and you can withdraw that consent later in the app. We do not authorize these providers to use your content to train their own models, and we have switched off the data-sharing and model-training options in our accounts with each of them. The providers may keep the data for a limited period under their own terms. For example, with our current Anthropic settings, inputs and outputs are kept for 30 days and may be accessed by Anthropic for safety and security purposes, and ElevenLabs keeps data by default under its own privacy policy.

Other service providers. Providers that help operate the app, including hosting, database and sign-in (Supabase, which also sends the password-reset email, and Render), email delivery (Resend), which handles the messages you send through the app's contact form, and push delivery for notifications (Expo). The app does not use analytics or advertising services, and it does not process payments yet. In-app purchases, when offered, are processed by Apple or Google.

Competent authorities: where required by law, under a binding order or decision, or where permitted and necessary to protect rights, individuals, or security.

Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or transfer of business operations, subject to applicable law and user notification where required.

Access to and sharing of information will be limited to relevant purposes and what is necessary, in accordance with applicable law.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to legal retention obligations and applicable law. We do not set a fixed retention period for your account data: it is kept until you delete your account.

How to delete your account: You can delete your account yourself in the app: open Profile and choose Delete My Account, and it is deleted straight away. You can also write to contact@theilovemethod.com from the email address of your account, and we delete it within 30 days. The steps are also on our page theilovemethod.com/delete-data. When you delete your account, your personal information, your voice recordings, your usage record and your reports about AI replies are deleted from our active systems, subject to information we are legally required or permitted to retain under applicable law.

Certain copies in backups and server logs may be retained for up to seven days, depending on the deletion procedures and the systems in which the information is stored. Server logs created before account deletion may still contain an account identifier, IP address, and request timestamps.

Anonymous and aggregated information that cannot reasonably be used to identify an individual may be retained for statistical purposes.

7. International Data Transfers

Your personal information may be processed or stored in countries outside your country of residence, depending on the locations of our service providers and infrastructure. In particular, the AI service providers named in section 5 are located in the United States.

Where personal information is transferred to a country that does not provide a level of protection recognized as adequate under applicable law, we will implement appropriate transfer mechanisms and safeguards as required, such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) under the GDPR where applicable.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Request restriction of processing.
  • Request portability of your personal information where this right applies.
  • Object to certain types of processing.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time where processing is based on consent, including your consent to share text with AI service providers. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

To exercise your rights, contact us at contact@theilovemethod.com.

We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority. In France, the competent authority is the CNIL (Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés).

9. Children's Privacy

The app is not intended for users under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

If you believe that a person under 18 has provided us with personal information, you may contact us at contact@theilovemethod.com. We will review the matter and act in accordance with applicable law, including deleting the information where required.

10. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Depending on the systems actually used, these measures may include:

  • Encryption of information in transit, such as through TLS.
  • Encryption of stored information where implemented in the relevant system.
  • Access controls limiting access to authorized personnel who need the information for their work and are subject to confidentiality obligations.
  • Security monitoring and periodic assessments.
  • Use of cloud infrastructure and service providers selected with security considerations in mind.

No system for transmitting or storing information can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

In the event of a personal data breach, we will comply with applicable notification and reporting obligations, including notifying the competent authority and affected users where required by law.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app, our data processing practices, or legal requirements.

Material changes will be communicated through an in-app notification or email, depending on the circumstances and applicable law. Where a change requires new consent, we will request that consent separately.

The date of the latest update will appear on the updated Privacy Policy.
